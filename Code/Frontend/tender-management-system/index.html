Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Tender Management System?

A Tender Management System is a specialized software platform designed to organize, estimate, track, and execute formal bidding processes. It replaces disconnected spreadsheets by centralizing tender intake, BoQ item costing, supplier quotations, bid security tracking, and contract execution in one unified system.

How does Mashrue manage Bill of Quantities (BoQ) estimation?

Mashrue breaks down complex BoQs into granular line items, allowing estimators to calculate supplier costs, customs duties, shipping charges, and desired profit margins. Real-time margin simulators alert teams if selling prices fall below landed costs.

How does the system track Earnest Money and Bid Securities?

Mashrue includes an audit-proof registry for Call Deposit Receipts (CDRs), Bid Bonds, and Bank Guarantees. It tracks issuing bank details, instrument numbers, lien amounts, custodians, and maturity dates to ensure deposits are reclaimed immediately upon tender award or cancellation.

Can Mashrue handle post-award contract fulfillment?

Yes. When a tender is won, Mashrue seamlessly converts the opportunity scope into client contracts, supplier purchase orders, multi-warehouse stock allocations, and official delivery challans.
