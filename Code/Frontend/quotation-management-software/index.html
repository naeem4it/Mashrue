Frequently Asked Questions

What is Quotation Management Software?

Quotation Management Software centralizes the creation, sending, tracking, and revision of commercial price quotations. It links purchasing costs with sales pricing to ensure every quote delivers healthy margins.

Can Mashrue collect and compare supplier RFQ rates?

Yes. Mashrue allows procurement and estimating teams to log multiple supplier quotations against specific line items, comparing unit prices, delivery lead times, and tax terms side-by-side.

Can an accepted quotation be converted into a Purchase Order or Invoice?

Yes. With a single click, an accepted quotation automatically generates customer sales orders, supplier purchase orders, and downstream delivery challans without re-typing data.
