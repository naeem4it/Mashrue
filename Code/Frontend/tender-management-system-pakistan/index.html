Frequently Asked Questions for Pakistani Businesses

How does Mashrue support PPRA rules in Pakistan?

Mashrue aligns with Federal PPRA and Provincial rules (Punjab PPRA, Sindh PPRA, KP PPRA, Balochistan PPRA). It tracks Single Stage One/Two Envelope procedures, tender opening dates, financial BoQ evaluations, and official evaluation reports.

How are Call Deposit Receipts (CDR) managed in Pakistan?

Pakistani procurement requires earnest money in the form of bank CDRs. Mashrue tracks CDR numbers, issuing bank branches, lien amounts, physical receipt holders, and alerts finance teams when security deposits are ready for release.

Is Mashrue certified with FBR PRAL digital invoicing?

Yes. Mashrue integrates with real-time FBR PRAL sales tax digital invoicing API. It issues verified QR-coded digital invoices, calculates sales tax and withholding deductions, and transmits cleared transactions to the FBR portal.
