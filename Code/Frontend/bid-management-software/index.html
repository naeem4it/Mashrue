Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bid Management Software?

Bid Management Software is a platform that automates the preparation, pricing, approval, and submission of commercial bids. It enables estimators and commercial managers to collaborate on technical proposals, ensure profitable margins, and track bid securities.

How does Mashrue prevent bidding at a financial loss?

Mashrue features an integrated margin simulation engine. As you price Bill of Quantities items, it calculates landed costs including supplier prices, taxes, customs, and transport fees. An automatic warning is triggered if your selling price drops below landed cost.

Can we manage Performance Bank Guarantees (PBG) in Mashrue?

Yes. Upon tender award, Mashrue tracks mandatory Performance Guarantees (PBGs), bank lien amounts, release milestones, and renewal dates to ensure complete compliance with client contract terms.
